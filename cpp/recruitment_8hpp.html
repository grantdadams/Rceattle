Calculates the recruitment for a given species and year.

Parameters
srr_funStock-recruit relationship (SRR) type: 0 = random about mean (the default), 2 = Beverton-Holt, 4 = Ricker. Codes 1, 3 and 5 are retired aliases of 0, 2 and 4 that compute the same curve here; build_srr() refuses them, because the environmental effect now comes from a recruitment linkage on R0 (code 1) or alpha (3 and 5).
R0Equilibrium or mean recruitment level (thousands of fish); read by the random-about-mean forms only.
ssbFemale spawning-stock biomass (mt), lagged by minage by the caller.
srr_alphaSRR productivity parameter.
srr_betaSRR density-dependence parameter.
rec_devAnnual recruitment deviation, log scale.
spr0Equilibrium spawning biomass per recruit at F = 0 (M of year 1). Unused here: both curves are functions of srr_alpha, srr_beta and ssb, and the caller derives the unfished recruitment they imply – (alpha - 1/spr0)/beta for Beverton-Holt, a posfun()-guarded log for Ricker.
Returns
Expected recruitment (thousands of fish).
