Calculates selectivity for every fleet, sex, bin (age or length) and year.

Builds each fleet's hindcast curve from its Selectivity form, applies the per-sex apical height, then normalizes and projects via normalize_and_project_selectivity(); convert_length_selectivity() carries a length-based curve to ages. flt_sel_dim (0 = age, 1 = length) picks the grid per case, so a length-based curve has no case of its own.

Selectivity forms

The codes are fleet_control$Selectivity; R/0-switches.R is the authority. The parametric forms (1, 3, 4, 8, 11, 15) take linkage offsets in the slots named at their case; 15 has no deviate array and varies only through linkages. The coefficient forms (2, 5, 6, 7, 9, 13) take annual deviates but no linkage offset – build_selectivity() refuses one, since mean-centring cancels a per-year offset. x below is the length-bin midpoint (cm) or, on an age-based fleet, the 1-based age-bin ordinal bin + 1 (LogisticPM uses bin + 1.5); minage is NOT added, so an inflection or peak is on the bin scale, not the age scale.

  • 0 Fixed: read from emp_sel_obs; age-based.
  • 1 Logistic: ascending logistic on log_sel_slp(0) and sel_inf(0).
  • 2 NonParametric (Ianelli et al. 2018, AMAK): one log-scale coefficient per bin, sel_coff bins 0..N_sel_bins-1 with the rest plateauing, mean-centred, and penalized for monotonicity and curvature.
  • 3 DoubleLogistic (Dorn and Methot 1990): ascending logistic x (1 - descending).
  • 4 DescendingLogistic: the descending limb alone, on log_sel_slp(1)/sel_inf(1).
  • 5 Hake (Taylor et al. 2014): cumulative sum of sel_coff from bin_first_selected up, exponentiated and normalized per sex to 1 at one sel_norm_bin1, else at its maximum.
  • 6 2DAR1: inverse-logit of sel_coff + sel_coff_dev, the deviates carrying an AR1 correlation across bin and year.
  • 7 3DAR1 (Cheng et al 2024): conditional-variance parameterisation of a 3D random field across age, cohort and year.
  • 8 DoubleNormal: four-parameter dome, Gaussian limbs blended by a steep logistic so the curve is twice differentiable, with a logit-scale right-tail floor analogous to SS3 pattern 24's P6.
  • 9 NonParametricPM (AMAK "pm"): random walk on the per-year-centred log-selectivity, held flat above flt_sel_cap_bin; its increments are penalized, not integrated, so it is not integrable under random_sel.
  • 11 LogisticPM (ADMB AMAK "pm" bottom-trawl survey): logistic with MULTIPLICATIVE slope and inflection deviates, plus a free first-bin log-selectivity.
  • 13 NonParametricIntegrable: the NonParametric curve whose deviates carry a proper density, so random_sel can integrate them; Time_varying_sel picks Off, IID or RandomWalk.
  • 15 DoubleNormalSS3: Stock Synthesis size pattern 24 (age pattern 20) on SS3's own six parameters – P1 peak (cm or age), P2 top width (logit), P3/P4 ascending and descending widths (log), P5/P6 initial and final selectivity (logit) – held in their own array sel_dn6. Unnormalized: the curve peaks at 1 by construction.

Converts one fleet/sex/year of length-based selectivity to age-based.

Integrates selectivity-at-length over the growth transition matrix Prob(Length | Age) on the POPULATION length grid both arrays live on, so the product is formed at the resolution the curve is defined on, not on a bin average.

Zeroes, normalizes and projects ONE fleet's selectivity (age or length based).

Bins below bin_first_selected are zeroed; the last hindcast year carries across the projection. Normalization runs only when Sel_norm_bin is set (default Off), never for forms 5, 11 and 15: scaling to 1 at sel_norm_bin1, to a mean of 1 over [bin1, bin2], or to the maximum when bin1 is negative. Under Sel_norm_scope = AcrossSexes the less-selected sex deliberately peaks below 1.
